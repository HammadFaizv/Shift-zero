**Low-Poly 3D Magnifying Glass** by [shevafleesinz](https://sketchfab.com/shevafleesinz).
[Original model](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/low-poly-3d-magnifying-glass-5c6a1f13ec5c4653aaf46492a467f236),
licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Attribution comes from the supplied GLB metadata. The game scales and rotates
the asset and makes its lens transparent; the source model remains intact.

The thirteen current evidence photographs in `Assets/evidence/` are editable SVG scenes
composed from the user-supplied modular pack in `Assets/evidence_assets/`, with
additional quote, strap and energy-beam vector details authored for this project.
The supplied pack contains no author or license information in its README or
level guide; its original files remain intact. Evidence lettering uses DejaVu
Sans outlines. Reading-glasses geometry and optical guides
are authored directly in the Godot scenes. The wood texture maps were supplied with
the project and remain in `Assets/materials/`.
